## **How to Play: Halloween Trick-or-Treating Optimization Game**

**OBJECTIVE:**  
Maximize your candy collection in 90 minutes of trick-or-treating!

**HOW TO PLAY:**  
Click on houses to visit them and collect candy. Each visit collects:
- **Fun-size candy** (pink houses): 1 piece, adds 1 weight unit
- **Full-size candy** (orange houses with ⭐): 3 pieces, adds 3 weight units

**CONSTRAINTS:**
- **Time Limit:** You have 90 minutes total
- **Travel Time:** Distance between houses divided by your current speed
- **Encumbrance:** As your bag gets heavier, you walk slower! Your speed = 60 × (1 - 0.0015 × weight) m/min
- **House Closings:** Each house closes at a specific time (check the countdown above each house)
- **Limited Candy:** Houses have limited quantities that deplete as game time passes—even if you don't visit them!
- **Collection Time:** Spending 2-3 minutes at each house (shown below the house)

**STRATEGY TIPS:**
- Plan your route carefully—heavy bags slow you down significantly
- Watch closing times—some houses close early!
- Candy is disappearing as time passes—don't wait too long!
- Balance distance, candy value, and your current speed

**END GAME:**  
The game ends when time runs out or all houses close. Your final score and complete route will be displayed. Can you beat your classmates' candy counts?
